What's Included in a Bend Furnace Tune-Up?

A proper tune-up goes far beyond just changing a filter. Our technicians follow a detailed checklist to ensure every component of your heating system is checked, cleaned, and optimized for the season ahead.

Heat Exchanger Inspection

Cracks in a heat exchanger can lead to dangerous carbon monoxide leaks. We carefully inspect this critical component to ensure your home's air is safe and healthy.

Burner & Pilot Cleaning

Dirty burners can cause inefficient combustion and uneven heating. We'll clean and adjust your burners and pilot light for maximum efficiency and reliability.

Blower Motor & Fan Check

We test the blower motor and fan assembly to ensure proper airflow through your ductwork. This prevents system strain and ensures even heat distribution.

Thermostat Calibration

We'll test and calibrate your thermostat to ensure it's accurately measuring the temperature in your home, preventing unnecessary cycling and energy waste.

Electrical Connection Check

Loose or frayed electrical connections are a major cause of system failure. We'll inspect and tighten all connections to ensure safe and reliable operation.

Carbon Monoxide Testing

Your safety is our top priority. We'll perform specialized testing to ensure your furnace is not releasing carbon monoxide into your living space.

Bend Furnace Tune-Up: Common Questions

Contact Us

Why should I get a furnace tune-up if my system seems fine?

Just like a car, your furnace needs regular maintenance to run its best. A tune-up catches minor wear and tear, prevents sudden breakdowns during freezing weather, maintains your manufacturer's warranty, and ensures your system is operating safely without any carbon monoxide risks.

How often should I schedule a tune-up for my heating system?

We recommend a professional tune-up once a year, ideally in the fall before you need to turn the heat on for the first time. This ensures your system is clean, safe, and ready for Central Oregon winter demands.

Will a tune-up really lower my monthly energy bills?

Yes. A clean system with properly adjusted burners and a well-maintained blower motor operates much more efficiently. This means it uses less gas or electricity to heat your home, resulting in noticeable savings on your monthly utility costs.

How long does a professional furnace tune-up take?

A comprehensive furnace tune-up typically takes between 60 and 90 minutes. Our technician will perform a multi-point inspection, clean essential components, and test all safety controls to ensure thorough results.

What's the difference between a tune-up and a repair?

A tune-up is proactive maintenance performed on a working system to prevent issues. A repair is reactive service performed to fix a system that has already failed or is malfunctioning. Regular tune-ups are the best way to avoid the need for emergency repairs.

Do you offer maintenance plans for Bend homeowners?

Yes! Our Comfort Club maintenance plans include two seasonal tune-ups (one for heating and one for cooling) per year, plus priority scheduling and member discounts on any repairs you might need.
