Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions About Commercial HVAC Repair in Phoenix, Answered

How much does commercial HVAC repair cost in Phoenix?

The cost of commercial HVAC repair depends on the unit size, location (rooftop or indoor), and the specific parts needed. We provide a thorough diagnostic visit and a clear, detailed quote before any work begins, ensuring you have a firm price for your records.

Do you offer emergency commercial HVAC repair?

Yes. We understand that a breakdown can impact your business operations. We offer priority dispatch for commercial clients and strive to get a technician to your site as quickly as possible to restore your building's climate control.

Can you repair rooftop units and split systems?

Absolutely. Our technicians are trained to repair all major types of commercial HVAC equipment, including packaged rooftop units (RTUs), large-scale split systems, and ductless solutions. We work with all major commercial brands.

Will I get an upfront estimate for the repair?

Yes. Once we identify the problem, we'll explain the solution and provide a firm, upfront price. You approve the repair and the cost before we start, so there are no surprises on your invoice.

Should I repair or replace our commercial unit?

We'll provide an honest assessment based on the unit's age, condition, and the cost of the repair versus a new installation. If the unit is over 15 years old and needs a major repair, a new energy-efficient replacement is often the more cost-effective choice.

Are your commercial HVAC repairs guaranteed?

Yes. We stand behind our workmanship and provide a 100% satisfaction guarantee on our commercial HVAC repairs. We ensure your system is operating safely and reliably before we consider the job complete.

How do I schedule a commercial HVAC repair?

To schedule a commercial HVAC repair in Phoenix, call us at (480) 427-5588 or fill out our online contact form. We'll find a convenient time to get your business back to a comfortable temperature.
