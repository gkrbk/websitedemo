Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions About Commercial HVAC Installation in Phoenix, Answered

How much does a new commercial HVAC installation cost in Phoenix?

The cost of a new commercial HVAC installation depends on the system type (RTU, split, etc.), tonnage, and efficiency rating. We provide free, detailed estimates where we evaluate your building's needs and recommend the best options for your budget. You'll receive a clear, upfront quote before any work begins.

How long does a typical commercial HVAC installation take?

Most commercial HVAC installations, including rooftop unit replacements, can be completed in a single day with proper planning. More complex systems or large-scale multi-unit projects may take longer. We'll provide a clear timeline during your estimate and coordinate around your business hours.

Do you install all major commercial HVAC brands?

Yes. We specialize in installing a wide range of top commercial HVAC brands, including Trane, Carrier, Lennox, and Goodman. We help you select the brand and model that best fits your building's specific requirements and efficiency goals.

Will I get an upfront estimate for the installation?

Absolutely. We provide free, detailed estimates for all new commercial HVAC installations. Our quotes include the cost of equipment, labor, any necessary crane services, and permits, ensuring total transparency with no hidden fees.

Should I replace all my commercial units at once?

While not always necessary, replacing multiple aging units simultaneously can often save on mobilization and crane costs. We'll evaluate the condition of all your units and give you an honest recommendation based on your long-term operational needs.

Is your commercial HVAC installation work guaranteed?

Yes. We stand behind our installation quality with a 100% satisfaction guarantee. Additionally, all new commercial equipment comes with manufacturer warranties, and we'll review all the details with you during your consultation.

How do I schedule a commercial HVAC installation estimate?

To schedule your free commercial HVAC installation estimate in Phoenix, call us at (480) 427-5588 or fill out our online contact form. We'll find a convenient time to discuss your business's comfort needs.
