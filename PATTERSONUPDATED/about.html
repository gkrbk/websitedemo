Air Conditioning and Heating Specialists

Expert HVAC Service for Sacramento Homes & Businesses

Patterson Heating & Air Conditioning is your local HVAC company serving Sacramento and surrounding communities with dependable, professional service you can count on. We are a local family-owned business based in Wilton/Elk Grove, CA, providing professional HVAC services across the greater Sacramento area for 32 years. Owner Chris Patterson began at age 19 as an installer and advanced through service technician, master technician, comfort advisor, and owner, which shapes our exceptional service, master craftsmanship, and expert technical knowledge that drives repeat customers and referrals. We train daily and only hire highly skilled technicians with attention to detail. We offer free estimates, fast service, top customer service, and factory-trained, authorized technicians with most factory parts on our trucks. We provide expert AC & heating repair, installation, maintenance, and many other HVAC services — completed correctly the first time, with careful attention to detail and clear communication throughout every job. Our technicians handle all major brands — Trane, Carrier, Lennox, Goodman, Daikin, and more — delivering accurate diagnostics, efficient solutions, and long-lasting results for any make or model. Whether you need fast help, a system upgrade, or routine upkeep, we’re here to make the entire process simple and stress-free. For fast, no-pressure service, contact us or call for a free quote at +1 (916) 275-3500.
