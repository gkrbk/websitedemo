Why Clean Coils Matter

Signs Your AC Needs Professional Coil Cleaning

Don't wait for your system to freeze up. Look out for these common indicators that your air conditioner's coils are overdue for a cleaning.

Higher Energy Bills

If your cooling costs have spiked without a change in usage, dirty coils may be forcing your system to consume more energy to reach the set temperature.

Warm Air Blowing

When the evaporator coil is insulated by dirt, it cannot absorb heat effectively, resulting in warm or lukewarm air blowing from your vents.

System Runs Constantly

A system struggling to cool due to dirty coils will run for longer cycles or continuously, causing unnecessary wear and tear on components.

Frozen Evaporator Coils

Restricted airflow from dirt and dust can cause condensation to freeze on the evaporator coil, completely halting the cooling process.

Frequent Breakdowns

The added strain caused by dirty coils often leads to compressor failure and other serious mechanical issues if left unaddressed.

Weak Airflow

A clogged coil acts as a barrier, significantly reducing the amount of air that can push through the system and into your living spaces.

How often should my AC coils be cleaned?

We recommend having your coils checked and cleaned at least once a year as part of a comprehensive annual AC tune-up. If you live in an area with high dust or have pets, more frequent cleaning may be necessary.

Can I clean the coils myself?

While you can gently clear loose debris from the outside of the condenser unit, deep cleaning requires chemical solutions and specialized tools. Attempting it yourself can easily bend the delicate aluminum fins or damage electrical components, so it's best left to professionals.

What happens if I never clean my coils?

Neglecting coil cleaning leads to decreased efficiency, higher energy bills, frozen coils, and eventually, catastrophic failure of the compressor, which is often the most expensive part to replace.

How long does a professional coil cleaning take?

A standard coil cleaning usually takes between 1 to 2 hours, depending on how accessible the coils are and the level of buildup. It is often performed as part of a full system maintenance visit.

Does coil cleaning improve system performance?

Yes! A dirty evaporator coil can become a breeding ground for mold and bacteria, which is then blown into your home. Cleaning the coil removes these contaminants, leading to fresher, healthier air.

Will cleaning my coils lower my electric bill?

Yes. By removing the insulating layer of dirt, your system doesn't have to work as hard or run as long to cool your home, which translates directly to lower energy consumption and reduced utility bills.
