Common HVAC Issues We Resolve in the Kansas City Northland

From poor airflow to inconsistent temperatures, HVAC systems can experience a variety of problems. Our expert technicians have the tools and experience to find the root cause and fix it fast.

Poor Airflow

Low airflow can be caused by a failing blower motor, clogged filters, or ductwork issues. We'll identify the restriction and restore powerful, efficient air movement.

Inconsistent Temperatures

If some rooms are hot while others are cold, your system might have balancing issues or a failing sensor. We'll recalibrate your system for even comfort across your home.

System Won't Cycle

When your HVAC system won't turn on or off, the cause is often a thermostat malfunction, electrical failure, or a safety limit switch. We'll find and fix the break in the cycle.

Strange Operational Noises

Hissing, banging, or screeching sounds indicate internal system strain. We'll perform a thorough inspection to identify failing mechanical parts before they cause a total breakdown.

High Energy Consumption

A sudden spike in utility bills often means your HVAC system is struggling to perform. We'll diagnose the efficiency loss and restore your system to its optimal performance level.

Water & Refrigerant Leaks

Leaking fluids can damage your home and your system's compressor. We are experts at identifying leak sources, performing lasting repairs, and recharging your system safely.

How do I know if my system needs repair or just maintenance?

Maintenance is proactive and includes cleaning and inspections. You need a repair if you notice performance changes like strange sounds, smells, poor airflow, or if your system stops cycling correctly. If your system isn't keeping your home at the set temperature, it's time for a professional repair diagnostic.

What is the cost of a typical HVAC repair in the Kansas City Northland?

The cost varies based on the component that failed and the complexity of the issue. At 816 Mechanical, Sam starts with a diagnostic visit to identify the problem, then provides a clear, upfront price. You'll know the exact cost and approve the work before any repairs begin.

Can you repair both my air conditioner and my furnace?

Yes. We specialize in both heating and cooling. Whether you have a split system, a package unit, or a heat pump, we can repair all components of your HVAC system.

How quickly can a technician arrive for an HVAC repair?

As a local Northland company, we prioritize prompt service and offer same-day options when available. We’re open Monday through Friday, 7:00am–6:00pm. Call (816) 323-9059 and we’ll share the next available arrival window.

Do you offer any guarantees on your HVAC repairs?

We stand behind our workmanship and aim for your full satisfaction. Specific manufacturer warranties vary by part; we’ll review details when we present your repair options.

Should I repair my old HVAC system or replace it?

We provide honest recommendations based on your system's age and condition. Generally, if a repair cost exceeds a large share of the value of a new system, or if your unit is over 12–15 years old, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term choice.
